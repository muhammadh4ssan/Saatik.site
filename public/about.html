About Saatik

Last updated: October 5, 2026

What Saatik is

Saatik is a free tool for saving public TikTok videos, sounds and photos for personal use. Paste a link, tap Download, and choose the format you need.

How it works

We look up the public file for the link you paste and pass it to your browser. You do not need to sign in, and we do not keep a library of the links or files you download.

What we do not do

We are not affiliated with TikTok or ByteDance. We cannot download private videos, and we do not host any videos ourselves.

Languages

The tools are available in English, Urdu, Hindi and Arabic.

Responsible use

Videos belong to their creators. Please download only what you have the right to save, and do not re-upload or sell other people's work.
